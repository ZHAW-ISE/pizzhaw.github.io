Get ready to put your cybersecurity skills to the test. The semesterly Capture-The-Flag (CTF) competition returns to Zurich University of Applied Sciences (ZHAW) on November 27, 2026.

Whether you are already experienced with system-level security or just looking to run your first exploits, this event is designed to provide hands-on security engineering training in a relaxed, collaborative environment. Participants will tackle a wide variety of technical challenges across categories including Web, Pwn, Reverse Engineering, Crypto, Forensics, and Misc.

A massive thank you to our event sponsor, [goSecurity](https://www.gosecurity.ch/), for supporting us for the evening. Their support ensures we can keep the focus on finding flags—and keep the pizza boxes full. We also want to extend a special thanks to our hosting provider, the [Swiss Hacking Challenge](https://www.swiss-hacking-challenge.ch/), and their team, **/mnt/ain**, for providing the reliable infrastructure needed to run our challenges smoothly.

**Event Details**

- **Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Time:** 5:30pm to open end (approximately 02:00am)
- **Location:** ZHAW Campus, Room TN E0.25 (Mensa next to the main entrance)
- **Who Can Join:** Open to students interested in IT security and software systems. Alumni also welcome to join. No prior experience is required, but a basic understanding of programming and computer systems will be helpful.
- **Catering:** Free pizza and drinks, generously sponsored by [goSecurity](https://www.gosecurity.ch/).
- **What to Bring:** Your own laptop. Ensure you have your preferred terminal environment, scripts, tools, and any necessary virtual machines or emulators ready to go.

**Solve challenges. Earn flags. Eat pizza. Repeat.**

Registration is officially open, but spots are limited to ensure everyone has a great experience and plenty of food. Secure your spot and register via the link below. Event is limited to 50 participants, so sign up early to avoid disappointment.
